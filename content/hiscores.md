{{< hiscores >}}

The hiscores are rebuilt every five minutes from what the game has saved, so a character that's being played shows its progress after its next save, up to about 20 minutes later. Guests and staff aren't on them. To take your character off, type `/hiscores off` in the game, and `/hiscores on` to put it back.
