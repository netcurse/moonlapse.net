Version 0.11.0 makes combat fairer and gives you more to use it on. A fight at your own level should now be a real contest, with food as the way to get through it. There are **three new quests**, Springport has turned green and filled with people, and there's some new lore to dig into.

## Fairer fights

Fights used to swing between hopeless and harmless on one tier of armour, and walking away could stop a monster hitting you at all. Neither is true now.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Walking isn't a defence** | A monster swings as soon as it's ready, even mid-chase. Melee enemies walk as fast as you, unless they're slow on purpose |
| **Chance to hit** | Worked out afresh, so each tier of armour helps without making you untouchable. A level 10 wolf hits about 47% of the time with no armour and 26% in bronze. Iron no longer takes it nearly to nothing |
| **Food** | Takes 1 to 3 seconds to eat, depending on what it heals, and being hit doesn't interrupt it. Your fight picks back up when you've finished |
| **Full** | Eat in a fight and you're **Full** for a while, and can't eat again until it wears off. The more of your health the food restored, the longer it lasts, up to a minute. Outside a fight, eat all you like |
| **Monsters that give up** | They heal over a few seconds instead of all at once, and you can still attack them on the way home |

**Mend is now Mend other**, and only heals another party member, so nobody has to train Magic to stay alive. **Recall** is a quick way out now: it takes four seconds to cast, and leaves you moonsick for ten seconds, not ten minutes.

## Springport, green again

Springport was a field of paving and sealed houses, with people who never moved. Now it's a city to walk round:

- **Grass and olive trees** inside the walls, with balsa and oak to chop outside them.
- **Nineteen houses** you can walk into, with people at home, and ten more citizens out in the streets.
- **People move about**, except those serving you across a counter.
- **Ciro's rickshaw** goes round the ring road. Five coins buys a seat for a lap of all eight districts, and he tells you about each one as you pass.
- **Barry's ferry** rows you across the estuary to Alan's camp for 30 coins.

## Shops and the Marketplace

- **Shops no longer undercut crafting.** Some sold things for less than their ingredients cost, leaving little reason to make or trade them. Most of the Armoury's iron gear has gone, along with raw catfish and bream and the Weavers' Hall's cloth, and 18 items have new prices.
- **Change a sell order's price** without cancelling it: press <kbd>p</kbd> on the order's details, at any clerk. It's free, and the order goes behind the others already at that price.
- **Guide prices are in the game.** An item's prices show its guide price, and the Sell tab has a Guide column.
- **Item descriptions** are on the [Marketplace page](/market/), under each item's name.

## And the rest

- **Choose who you're talking to.** <kbd>Tab</kbd> on the chat line moves between everyone near, your party and the last player you whispered with, and the prompt says which. No need to remember `/p` or `/w`.
- **Look back at your letters.** A mailbox's window has a **Sent** tab (<kbd>Tab</kbd>) with your newest 20. Your copy stays even if the other player deletes theirs.
- **Springport's wines do something.** A cup or a bottle of red makes you heavy-handed, a bottle of white numbs you, and the bottles can be drunk socially.
- **Clearer messages.** It's "You can't reach Perla" now, not "the Perla", and "You receive 100 gold coins", without the capital. Crafting says what you're doing ("You begin smelting a bronze bar...") and counts only what you made.
- **Caravan guards** no longer step into the water at the King's Bridge.

## Updating

**Version 0.10.0 no longer connects**, and the game will ask you to update. Download 0.11.0 from the [download page](/download/) and replace your old folder, copying across `guest.toml` if you play as a guest. The browser version is already up to date.
