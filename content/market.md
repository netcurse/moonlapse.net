Prices are what players have paid each other at the Marketplaces, before shipping. An item's guide price is the middle price of its sales over the last seven days, counted by the item, once it has had at least five sales between three or more players. Sales between one player's own characters, staff's sales, and sales far above what an item is worth don't count. Prices are worked out again every five minutes; what's for sale is as it is now. Nobody can see who bought or sold.
